Meet the people behind Saily

We’ve traveled enough to know how frustrating staying connected abroad can be. That’s why we built Saily — the eSIM app that makes staying connected simple for everyone.

Why we built Saily

People are natural explorers, and crossing the seas has always been the ultimate adventure. For many years, sailing was how they did it. Today, you may not be crossing the ocean to find a new continent, but the urge to travel and explore hasn’t gone away. And that's what we're all about.

Saily gives you the tools to explore the world in a modern way. We’re building a future where connectivity is effortless, instant, and entirely digital. No plastic. No borders. Just tap, connect, and go.

The Saily tale

We envisioned a better way for travelers to stay online. Today, we’re making that vision a reality for thousands around the globe. Here’s how Saily got here.

Where it all began

The Saily eSIM app started with a shared idea — ditch plastic SIM cards and simplify mobile connectivity.

November 2023

The first launch

We introduced our first eSIM plans that covered 160+ countries with instant mobile access and zero plastic waste.

March 2024

Prioritizing security

We’ve added built-in security features to our eSIM app so you can browse more safely while you travel.

December 2024

Connecting the world

At the start of the year, we expanded our coverage to 200+ destinations and reached more people than ever before.

February 2025

The journey continues

We’re focused on expanding our coverage, improving the Saily experience, and keeping you connected 24/7.

2025
